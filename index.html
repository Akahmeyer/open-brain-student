YouTube transcript

Paste a video link. Your app can't fetch YouTube subtitles by itself yet (that becomes automatic in Level 3), so for now you copy the transcript from YouTube and paste it here.

How to copy the transcript:
  1. Click Get Transcript below. The video opens in a new tab.
  2. Under the video, click the description (...more), scroll down and click Show transcript. On a phone, tap the description, then Show transcript.
  3. A transcript panel opens. Click and drag from the first line to the last line to select it, then copy (Ctrl+C / Cmd+C).
  4. Come back here and paste it in the box below. Timestamps are removed automatically.
